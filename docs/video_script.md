# Demo video script (hard cap 3:00)

Plain English narration at about 150 words a minute (about 375 words in total, no segment faster than 150 words a minute, which leaves room for the screens to breathe). Every number spoken or shown comes from the README. Screens from the hosted demo show the "Demo data" badge; numbers on demo screens (DHU, counts, alert text) are demo data and are not narrated as results.

Optional Telugu lines are marked **[Telugu, optional]**. They are drafts: check the wording before recording and show English subtitles.

| Time | Shot (on screen) | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:20 | The unit in Kadiri: rows of machines, a tailor sewing a shirt collar. Title card: "StitchCheck". | This is a new garment unit in Kadiri, Andhra Pradesh. Twenty-five machines, skilled tailors, and no track record. Before any buyer gives it work, it has to prove its quality. Today that proof is a paper checklist. It misses defects, and the buyer cannot check any of it. |
| 0:20 to 0:50 | The checker taps Try demo, then photographs a collar seam close-up. Step 1 card: the crop and the classifier's label. Tap: the photo with the seam tinted green. Then Muse Spark's label, severity and one-line reason. | The checker holds the phone close to the seam, because stitch defects are tiny. SAM 3.1 finds the seam, shown in green. Our classifier, fine-tuned on stitch images, flags it in a fraction of a second. Muse Spark then calls the classifier as a tool, compares the seam with a good reference seam, and explains its call in one line. The checker taps Agree. |
| 0:50 to 1:10 | A second photo with the "Your call" badge. The checker holds the mic button and speaks. The voice sheet shows the transcript and the parsed label; the checker taps Confirm. | When the AI and the classifier disagree, the photo comes to the checker. Here the checker corrects one call, in Telugu. Muse Spark maps the words onto the fixed lists, and one tap confirms it. |
| | **[Telugu, optional]** The checker speaks: "ఇది మరక కాదు, కుట్టు తెగింది." Subtitle: "This is not a stain, the stitch is broken." | |
| 1:10 to 1:20 | Expand the trace once: `classify_stitch`, its top-2 labels with probabilities, then the final answer. Tap the language button in the header: the whole app switches to Telugu and back. | Every decision keeps its trace: the tool call, what it returned, and the answer. And one button switches the whole app between Telugu and English. |
| 1:20 to 1:55 | Dashboard: DHU and defective % on top, bars by machine. A trend alert for one machine; tap it to open the mechanic's work order in Telugu and English. Then tap the pass chance to show the AQL 2.5 audit plan. | Every confirmed defect lands on the dashboard, by machine, never by person. When one machine keeps repeating a defect, a trend alert fires, and Muse Spark drafts a work order for the mechanic: needle, tension, timing, in Telugu and English. The supervisor also sees the chance the lot would pass a buyer's AQL 2.5 audit at today's defect rate. |
| 1:55 to 2:15 | Report screen: tap Generate. The buyer report with supervisor actions in Telugu and English; open the trace with `get_dhu`, `get_top_issues`, `get_audit_pass_chance`. | At the end of the shift, Muse Spark writes the buyer report from tool results only. The server fills in every number from logged data, and a validator rejects any number the model typed. So the buyer can check every line. |
| 2:15 to 2:30 | More screen: the AI mode switch with its two figures. Tap "High sensitivity (Muse Spark Open)". | One tap switches to Muse Spark Open. On the test set it catches 94.4% of major defects instead of 92.9%, but flags 20.2% of good seams instead of 9.5%. It is also the automatic fallback. |
| 2:30 to 2:50 | The README results: the StitchingNet row (92.9%), then the phone table (85.8% caught, 93.8% of good seams flagged). | Now the honest part. On lab images it catches 92.9% of major defects. On 185 real phone photos from Kadiri, it flags 86% of major defects, but also 94% of good seams. So on a phone today, the AI suggests and the checker decides. Every confirmed photo becomes training data. |
| 2:50 to 3:00 | "Built during the hackathon" list from the README, then an end card: the Meta models in one line, the repo link. | Built during the hackathon with Muse Spark, Muse Spark Open, SAM 3.1 and Muse Voice Transcribe. StitchCheck: proof of quality, from a phone. |

## Recording notes

- Gallery shots (Try demo, then the gallery): for the 0:20 Agree shot use "Skipped stitch, cotton fabric" (a lab image; Muse Spark answers skipped stitch). For the 0:50 correction use "Broken stitch, white cotton" (a Kadiri phone photo; the AI suggests stain, as it does on most phone photos), and correct it by voice. Both answers are cached, so they come back in about 2 s.
- Keep the total at or under 3:00. Cut shots before cutting the 2:30 honest-results segment.
- Show "Demo data" on every hosted-demo screen. Never show a ranking of workers or a per-operator view.
- Captions on throughout. Numbers as digits on screen, matching the README.
- End card text: "StitchCheck. Muse Spark, Muse Spark Open, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice Transcribe. [repo link]".
